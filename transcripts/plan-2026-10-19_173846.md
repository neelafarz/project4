**Virtual Hallo-weenie** is a Halloween web app for phones, meant as a promotion that makes people want to visit a theme park. It's built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and it's pitched at ages 10 and up (spooky and playful, no gore).

**What exists now (Phase 1 prototype):**
- **Start screen:** it has a "Reduce motion" checkbox, preset from the phone's setting, and an Enter button. Enter starts the audio and the animation. It also has a credits list.
- **One vertical scene:** a moonlit hollow with a haunted house as the focal point. The three environmental features are the house, a graveyard and a pumpkin patch.

- **Story 1, "The Patch Awakens":** the player taps the three pumpkins from smallest to largest.
  - Each tap gives its own note, a glow ring and a progress dot.
  - A wrong order resets the progress.
  - After the third tap, a story of about 7.5 seconds plays. The pumpkins light up, the house windows flare, and bats burst out. The pumpkins glow brighter and cackle, then fizzle into smoke and return to normal.

- **Locked and decoy interactions:** all other taps are disabled while a story plays. Tapping the door or the gravestones gives sound and visual feedback but doesn't start anything.

- **Play/Pause:** one button freezes the sound, animation, timers and interaction together. The `P` key does the same.
- **Accessibility:** touch and keyboard both work, and tap targets are at least 44px. Reduced motion removes the bats, leaves, drifting and flashing, and leaves only glows and fades.
